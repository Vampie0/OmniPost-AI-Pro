# 🖥️ SocialPilot AI Pro — Web Admin Master Guide

The **Web Admin Control Center** is the central control room for template buyers and business owners.

---

## 🎨 1. White-Label Engine (`/white-label`)
- **Brand Colors**: Change Primary, Secondary, and Accent colors via the color picker. Changes are pushed to mobile clients **in real-time**.
- **Product Name & Logo**: Update the application title and branding asset URLs instantly.
- **Feature Flags**: Enable or disable RevenueCat subscriptions, social logins, or maintenance mode.

---

## 🤖 2. AI Engine Configuration (`/ai-settings`)
- **Copywriting Models**: Switch between Google Gemini (Default) and OpenAI GPT-4o.
- **Image Generation**: Configure Replicate (SDXL / Flux), Stability AI, or DALL-E 3.
- **System Prompts**: Edit the master copywriting personality and tone used across all user prompts.

---

## 👥 3. User & Subscription Management (`/users` & `/subscriptions`)
- View all registered users, roles, and real-time subscription statuses.
- One-click credit adjustments (+50 / -50 credits).
- Instant user suspension (forces immediate logout on the mobile client).

---

## 🔔 4. Push Broadcasts (`/notifications`)
- Dispatch instant push notifications to all users or target paid Pro/Agency subscribers.
